Frequently Asked Questions

Common enquiries regarding NestFlex Architectural Modular Systems.

What is the standard build time for a NestFlex home?
Our factory build cycle is typically 12 weeks, with on-site installation and finishing taking between 1 to 3 weeks depending on the complexity of the site and the specific system chosen.
Are these homes compliant with South African building regulations?
Yes, all NestFlex systems are LSF (Lightweight Steel Frame) certified and exceed national SANS 10400 energy and structural standards, including thermal efficiency and fire protection requirements.
Do you provide financing for modular homes?
We facilitate finance applications through our partner networks. Because our systems carry structural warranties and full certifications, they are increasingly recognized by major South African financial institutions for residential bonding.
Can I recover VAT on my modular home investment?
Qualifying commercial entities and property investors may be eligible for VAT recovery (currently 15%) on their modular housing assets. We recommend consulting with your tax advisor to confirm eligibility for your specific investment structure.
What kind of warranty is provided?
NestFlex systems come with a Lifetime Structural Warranty on the core steel framework, giving you peace of mind regarding the longevity and durability of your real estate asset.
